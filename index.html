About

A studio built like an instrument

Locked In exists for the session that needs every detail right — the room, the signal path, and the two feet between the mic and the vocalist.

Founded on the belief that a studio is only as good as the ears running it, Locked In pairs acoustically treated rooms with an in-house plugin line, so the sound you chase in the box is the same sound the room was built to catch.
